# Product notes

## Problem

Pet owners often struggle to find a name that feels right — unique enough, easy to call, and matching the dog's personality or breed.

## Audience

- New dog owners and adopters
- Families deciding together
- Breeders / shelters who suggest starter names

## Core loop

1. Land and set a few preferences (optional)
2. Explore names with filters and inspiration cards
3. Save favorites into a shortlist
4. Share or finalize a name

## Open questions

- Curated editorial list vs community-submitted names?
- Free-only vs soft monetization (premium packs, AI naming)?
- Mobile-first PWA or full native later?
